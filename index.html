The problem

Your work lives everywhere. Your attention lives in one place.

Messages, tasks, meetings, project files and client feedback all arrive in different apps. Before any real work starts, you spend the first part of every day finding where you left off, what changed overnight, and who is waiting on you.

Cloud assistants could help, but most of them see everything you send them, and for many companies that rules them out. Peek-A-Bot reads it all on your Mac, and when it needs more power, its Private Cloud is built so no one can see what it processes.

How it learns

It doesn’t guess how you work. It learns.

The model never trains on your data. Instead it reads your history once and writes a private profile that it checks every time it acts. A model with a good profile of you beats a huge model that knows nothing about you.
  1. 1

    Connect

    Mail, Teams or Slack, Wrike or Jira, your calendar and your project folders. Exports work too, so you don’t need IT to approve an integration first.

  2. 2

    Learn overnight

    It reads about six months of history, once, and writes your Me file: people, projects, open promises, your voice, your rhythm.

  3. 3

    Act

    Nudges, drafts and workspace setup, each done the way you did it last time, because the Me file is part of every decision.

  4. 4

    Earn trust

    It starts by showing and asking. It only does more on its own after you’ve said yes to the same thing a few times.

What is Peek-A-Bot?

A small hand-drawn robot on a spring that lives above your Dock. It learns how you work from your own history, then sets up your day, tells you what matters, drafts replies in your voice and keeps you company. It runs on your Mac, with an encrypted Private Cloud for when you want more power.

Can I use it today?

Peek-A-Bot is in early access. The companion and its moves already run on the Mac; learning, the assistant features and Private Cloud reach early-access desks first. Join early access.

How does it learn about me?

You connect your sources once: mail, chat, your task tool, your calendar and the folders you work in. Overnight it reads about six months of history and writes your Me file: the people you work with, your projects, open promises, your writing voice, your rhythm, and how you name and share files.

Does anything leave my Mac?

Not unless you choose Private Cloud. By default your mail, chats, files and Me file are read and stored on your Mac, and there’s no Peek-A-Bot server holding them. How we handle your data.

What is Private Cloud?

More power when you want it: bigger models for heavy jobs, and a way to run Peek-A-Bot on Macs that can’t run a local model. It’s built so no one can see your data: requests are encrypted on your Mac, processed in sealed hardware with no admin access, erased the moment they’re answered, and not linked to who sent them.
