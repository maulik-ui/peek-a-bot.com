The moves

14 moves, and counting.

Every move is hand-timed, rubber-hose animation at 24 frames a second: gummy blocks that squash and stretch, a spring that rings and settles. Each one starts and ends on the same pose, so they chain together without a cut. Tap any of them to play it again.

Greetings

Hello, attention, welcome back.

Wave

Spots you, squats, three big slinky-arm waves.

Plays when you say hi, or it starts up

Jack-in-the-box

Crouch, rocket up out of the box, smack-down landing.

Plays when something needs you, or a surprise

Peek-a-boo

Hides its eyes, giggles, peeks out twice.

Plays when it feels playful, or you come back

Ambient

Life while nothing is happening; the idle timer picks from these.

Idle

One relaxed breath with a soft bob; loops cleanly.

Plays when nothing is happening

Curious peek

Hears something, leans left, leans right, shrugs.

Plays when nothing is happening, or it hears something

Snooze

Yawns, nods off with Zs, startles awake.

Plays when you step away, or it has been quiet a while

Status

What the assistant is doing: working, thinking, a message arrived.

Working

Hammers a toy keyboard, tongue out, final flourish.

Plays when you start focusing, or it is working for you

Think

Taps its chin, ponders, lightbulb idea.

Plays when it is thinking, or a draft is on the way

Message

Catches an envelope, peeks inside, shows it off.

Plays when news arrives, or a message lands

Wellbeing

Nudges: drink water, stretch, take a break.

Hydrate

Drinks from a coral bottle: you should too.

Plays when time for water

Stretch

Arms out wide, twists, big exhale.

Plays when time to stretch, or you have sat too long

Break

Sips from a mug, melts, perks up, waves.

Plays when time for a break, or your focus timer ends

Reactions

Responses to outcomes: success, confusion.

Celebrate

Cheers, two big hops, jelly shimmy, sparkles.

Plays when you finish something, or something works

Confused

Head tilt, question mark, big shrug.

Plays when something went wrong, or it needs your help

Coming next

A Hide move, where it ducks out of sight for quiet hours, and Peek under the edge, where it pops up from below the screen when something needs you. Most nudges get their own move, so the library keeps growing.
