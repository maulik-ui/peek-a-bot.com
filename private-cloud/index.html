Private Cloud

More power.
The same privacy.

Your Mac does everything it can on its own. When a job is too big for it, or your Mac can’t run a local model at all, Peek-A-Bot can send that task to Private Cloud: servers built so that no one can see your data. Not other customers, not cloud providers, not us.

Who it’s for

When your Mac needs a hand.

Your Mac can’t run a model

Older or lighter Macs don’t have the memory for a capable local model. Private Cloud gives them the same Peek-A-Bot, without the trade-off on privacy.

You want more power

Bigger models for the heavy lifting: a 40-page contract summarised, a quarter of email searched, a long report drafted in your voice.

Your work goes deeper

Multi-step workflows that would keep your Mac busy for an hour: research across folders, tidying a year of files, preparing a whole week.

How a request travels

Locked on your Mac. Unlocked only inside sealed hardware.

At no point in between can anyone read it, and at the end, nothing is left behind.
  1. 1

    Your Mac

    Picks only what the task needs from your files and Me file, then encrypts it.

  2. 2

    Independent relay

    Passes it on without knowing who sent it. It can’t read what it carries.

  3. 3

    Sealed server

    Proves which software it runs, decrypts inside sealed hardware, does the work.

  4. 4

    Back to you

    The answer comes back encrypted. The server erases everything it saw.

Six promises

What makes it private, not just encrypted.

Encrypted on your Mac first

Before a request leaves your Mac, it’s encrypted to keys that exist only inside our verified servers. The networks, relays and data centres in between carry it, but can’t read it.

Processed in sealed hardware

Requests run inside confidential-computing hardware: memory is encrypted and isolated from everything else on the machine. There’s no admin login and no debugging access, so nobody can look inside while it works, including our own engineers.

Nothing is kept

Each request is used once, to answer you, then erased. Your content is never written to disk, never logged, and never used to train anything.

Your Mac checks before it talks

Every server proves cryptographically which software it’s running. If it isn’t the exact build we’ve published, your Mac refuses to send it anything.

Not tied to you

Requests travel through an independent relay that hides your IP address, and are authorised with anonymous tokens, so a request can’t be traced back to the person who sent it.

Only what the task needs

Your Me file stays on your Mac. Peek-A-Bot sends only the pieces a task actually needs, and shows you what’s going before it goes.

Don’t take our word for it.

Every server build we run will be published, so independent security researchers can inspect the software and confirm that it does what this page says. Your Mac only talks to servers running one of those builds.

You’re in control

Three settings. You pick.

Private Cloud is off until you turn it on. On the Team plan, IT decides for everyone.

Always on my Mac

Nothing ever goes to the cloud. The default on every plan.

Ask me each time

Peek-A-Bot tells you when a job would be better in Private Cloud, and what it would send. You say yes or no.

Automatic for big jobs

Heavy tasks go to Private Cloud on their own; everything else stays local. You can see every job it sent.

Private Cloud comes with Pro, and can be switched on or off for a whole company on Team.
