Where we are

Proving it on one desk first.

We’d rather get one person’s day exactly right than everyone’s day roughly right. Here’s the order things arrive in.

  1. Today

    The companion

    Peek-A-Bot runs on the Mac with its library of hand-animated moves: it waves, idles, naps, reacts, and sits above your Dock.

  2. Next

    It starts learning

    Email import, the Me file, and the first five nudges, used every day by the team building it.

  3. Then

    “Set up my workspace”

    Chat, task-tool and folder indexing, and one-click workspace setup, piloted with a small group of early members.

  4. After that

    Launch and Private Cloud

    Personal and Pro plans, Private Cloud for big jobs and lighter Macs, a Windows version, and learned routines with the trust ladder.

  5. Later

    Teams

    The Team plan with IT controls, and ready-made know-how for more kinds of work.

Plans and dates are targets, not commitments.
